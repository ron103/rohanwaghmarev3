03 · sitewide system

Things that live on every page

  • Grain: her exact technique, a white page with a fixed SVG-noise layer at 16% (multiply in light mode, screen in dark). Paper Shaders' grain gradient adds extra atmosphere behind the hero and footer.
  • Cursor system (fine pointers only):
    • A blue dot that trails the real cursor on a spring.
    • A context label that streams in word by word ("open case study", "drag", "copy").
    • SVG cursors for each context: pointer, grab, crosshair over charts and graphs, text-select over code.
    • On touch devices all of this turns off.
  • Magnetic targets: primary buttons and the dock icons lean a few pixels toward the cursor and spring back.
  • Status line in Pacific time: like her time-of-day table, in Rohan's voice. "Probably asleep. The agents are still running." "Waiting on a training run." "Second chai." Placeholder lines until you write them.
  • Live presence: "3 people here right now", drawn as small nodes in the logo mark that link together as people arrive.
  • Top bar: the progressive blur and tuck-on-scroll. The active page gets a blue diamond.
  • ⌘K command palette: jump to any page, copy the email, switch theme, open the resume, and hidden commands like sudo hire rohan.
  • Page transitions: View Transitions, so a card's title morphs into the next page's H1.
  • Image loading: the tile-pop reveal on every project image and video.
  • Footer:
    • The sticky reveal under the rounded sheet.
    • A graph of nodes that light up and link toward the pointer. A click fires a "message" that hops along the edges, like agents passing work.
    • The giant per-letter email waves on hover and copies on click, with a toast.
  • Sound (optional, off by default): soft ticks on copy, regenerate and the command palette. A speaker toggle sits in the footer.

05 · use of space

Her spacing, as rules we can reuse

  • One container: 1440px max, 40px gutters (20px on mobile). Inner pages use a 200px sticky index, a 72px gap and a reading column of 520–620px.
  • Large rhythm: 160px between home sections, 64px between items in a section, and 224px of space above the hero on desktop. The empty space is part of the design.
  • Small rhythm: a 4px base. Labels sit 8px above their values, cards use 20–28px of padding, and tight groups use 12–16px gaps.
  • One idea per screen: each section fits about one viewport, with one strong element (a card, a quote row or a graph) and a quiet label.
  • Hierarchy without boxes: weight, size and color carry it. Cards are flat fills or 1px outlines, and shadows only appear on things that float.
  • Reading width: about 65 characters for prose and 20–28 words per tagline. Headlines use text-wrap: balance.

08 · guardrails

So it stays fast and kind

  • Lazy-load the heavy pieces (physics, diagrams, Pretext, shaders, terminal) per page, and only when visible. Target under about 180KB of JS on first load.
  • Mouse-only effects sit behind @media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine). Touch gets taps and the dock.
  • Reduced motion: every effect has a still end state, as on her site.
  • Keyboard: everything draggable also works with arrow keys, and every moment has a visible focus ring.
  • Every page is complete at rest: the content reads fine before any animation runs.
