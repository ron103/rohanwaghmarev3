04 · signature moves

Kept from the reference

  • A 1440px container, 40px gutters, 160px between sections, and lots of air
  • A serif-for-voice / sans-for-UI split, with light-weight serif for big lines
  • Flat cards (a fill or a 1px outline) and shadows only on floating things
  • A top bar with a progressive blur that tucks away on scroll; a floating dock on mobile
  • A content sheet with rounded bottom corners that reveals a dark footer
  • One hover language, one focus ring, press-scale, and a reduced-motion fallback for everything
  • A sticky left sub-nav on inner pages

New, and only Rohan's

  • Streaming tagline: lines type in token by token behind a blue block caret, the way an LLM streams
  • Before → after metrics: the old value struck through in mono, the new one in ink. All of his wins are shaped like this.
  • Trace waterfalls: live-coded span diagrams in place of product screenshots
  • Highlighter marks: yellow behind key phrases instead of a red tint
  • Node-graph mark: three linked nodes (a nod to his knowledge graphs) used as the logo, section bullet and active-nav marker
  • Status pill: "shipping at SAP Labs" with a heartbeat dot, in place of visitor presence
  • Night footer: ink-blue, with a node graph that drifts and links up near your cursor, and the email as a CLI call
